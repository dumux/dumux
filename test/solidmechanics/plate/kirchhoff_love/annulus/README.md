# Annular Kirchhoff-Love Plate with a Free Edge {#benchmark-annulus-free-edge}

**Problem Description**

This benchmark tests the free-edge boundary conditions of the mixed Kirchhoff-Love
formulation. An annulus $a < r < b$ is clamped at the inner edge and free at the
outer edge, and carries a uniform out-of-plane load $F$,

$$-D\,\nabla^4 w = F \quad \text{in } \Omega,$$

$$w = 0,\quad \partial_r w = 0 \quad \text{at } r = a, \qquad
  M_{rr} = 0,\quad V_r = 0 \quad \text{at } r = b.$$

**The free edge in the mixed formulation**

The mixed formulation replaces the fourth-order equation by five second-order
equations for $(\varphi, w, \psi)$ and $\boldsymbol{\theta}$ (see the
@ref KirchhoffLovePlate "model documentation"). The tensor

$$\mathbf{T} = \mathbf{M}(\boldsymbol{\theta}) - \varphi\mathbf{I} - \psi\mathbf{J}$$

is divergence-free, and its boundary traction has the components
$\mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{T}\mathbf{n} = M_{nn} - \varphi$ and
$\mathbf{s}\cdot\mathbf{T}\mathbf{n} = M_{ns} - \psi$, with $\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{J}\mathbf{n}$.
Prescribing

$$\mathbf{T}\mathbf{n} = -\varphi\,\mathbf{n}$$

as the traction of the rotation sub-problem therefore imposes $M_{nn} = 0$, the moment
condition of a free edge, and identifies $\psi$ with the twisting moment $M_{ns}$.
The Helmholtz decomposition $\mathbf{q} = \nabla\varphi + \mathbf{J}\nabla\psi$ gives
$\partial_n\varphi = q_n + \partial_s\psi$, so with $\psi = M_{ns}$ the natural flux of
the $\varphi$ equation is the Kirchhoff effective shear

$$\partial_n\varphi = q_n + \partial_s M_{ns} = V_n,$$

and a zero Neumann condition on that equation is the shear condition of a free edge.
The remaining two equations of the deformation sub-problem are natural as well: a zero
flux of the $w$ equation states $\boldsymbol{\theta}\cdot\mathbf{n} = \partial_n w$, and
the flux of the $\psi$ equation is prescribed with its own value, which leaves the curl
constraint unconstrained on the boundary.

The free edge also fixes the two gauge freedoms of the decomposition. A constant shift
of $\psi$ changes $\mathbf{T}\mathbf{n}$ by $-c\,\mathbf{s}$ and is therefore excluded,
so the interior constraint that pins $\psi$ in the fully clamped problem is not used here.
A harmonic shift of $\varphi$ with its conjugate shift of $\psi$ is excluded by the same
condition together with $\varphi = 0$ at the clamped edge.

On an annulus $\psi$ is single-valued only if the shear flux and $\partial_n\varphi$
carry the same net flux around the hole. Both fluxes vanish at the outer edge, so
their divergence equations give the same total flux through the inner edge.

**Analytical Solution**

The general axisymmetric solution of $-D\nabla^4 w = F$ is

$$w(r) = -\frac{F r^4}{64 D} + C_1 + C_2 r^2 + C_3\ln r + C_4 r^2\ln r,$$

and the four boundary conditions determine the constants,

$$C_4 = \frac{F b^2}{8D},$$

$$2C_2(1+\nu) - \frac{C_3(1-\nu)}{b^2}
  = \frac{F b^2(3+\nu)}{16D} - C_4\left[2(1+\nu)\ln b + 3 + \nu\right],$$

$$2C_2 a + \frac{C_3}{a} = \frac{F a^3}{16D} - C_4 a\left(2\ln a + 1\right),$$

$$C_1 = \frac{F a^4}{64D} - C_2 a^2 - C_3\ln a - C_4 a^2\ln a.$$

**Parameters**

| Parameter | Symbol | Value | Unit |
|-----------|--------|-------|------|
| Young's modulus | $E$ | $10^6$ | Pa |
| Poisson ratio | $\nu$ | 0.3 | - |
| Plate thickness | $t$ | 0.05 | m |
| Load | $F$ | 1.0 | N/m² |
| Inner radius | $a$ | 0.5 | m |
| Outer radius | $b$ | 1.0 | m |

**Setup**

The domain is an annulus discretized with an unstructured triangular mesh generated by
Gmsh from `annulus.geo`, whose aspect ratio $\alpha = a/b$ can be set with
`-setnumber alpha`. The discretization is the same as for the clamped disk: the Box
scheme for $(\varphi, w, \psi)$ and the PQ1-Bubble scheme for $\boldsymbol{\theta}$.

**Results**

From the build directory, run the convergence test with

```bash
ctest --output-on-failure -R '^test_kirchhoff_love_plate_annulus$'
```

To regenerate the figures, run from `test/solidmechanics/plate/kirchhoff_love/annulus`
inside the build directory:

```bash
python3 ../../plot_convergence.py ./test_kirchhoff_love_plate_annulus \
    --geo ../../annulus.geo --mesh annulus.msh --clmax 0.125 0.0625 0.03125 0.015625
```

Both the deflection and the shear gradient potential converge with second order, at mean
rates 1.98 and 1.99 over four meshes. The potential is the sharper of the two here: with the
free edge, $\varphi$ solves $\Delta\varphi = F$ with $\partial_n\varphi = 0$ at $r=b$ and the
gauge $\varphi = 0$ at $r=a$, so

$$\varphi(r) = \frac{F}{4}(r^2 - a^2) - \frac{F b^2}{2}\ln\frac{r}{a},\qquad
  \varphi'(r) = \frac{Fr}{2} - \frac{Fb^2}{2r},$$

and that derivative is identically the shear resultant $q_r = -D\,\partial_r\Delta w$ of the
closed form above. Convergence to it is therefore a direct check of
$\partial_n\varphi = V_n$, the identity the free edge rests on, and not only of the
deflection.

The relative $L_2$ error of $w$ converges with second order. At $\alpha = 0.5$ and
mesh size $h = 0.021$ the error is $2.6\times 10^{-4}$. The deflection of the free
edge, which is the quantity a wrinkling threshold is expressed in, is reported
separately; at mesh size `clmax` $= 0.03$ it agrees with the closed form to
$0.018\,\%$ at $\alpha = 0.25$, $0.045\,\%$ at $\alpha = 0.5$ and $0.21\,\%$ at
$\alpha = 0.75$.
